why it exists

warehouse-systems · executive briefing · November 2026inbound-receiving
Owns the inbound dock workflow up to, but not including, stock placement: the supplier's advance ship notice (ASN), the carrier's booked dock door window and the counted physical receipt whose close raises Short/Over/Damaged discrepancies. Fills the gap between 'a truck is announced' and 'units are staged in inventory-storage'; stock hands over purely event-driven via ReceiptLineReceived (Good quantities only) — no synchronous call in either direction. Hierarchical SSCC carton model supports case-level scan receiving and printable carton labels. Study project practicing DDD and hexagonal architecture within the warehouse-systems fleet.
what it does

Six jobs take a truck from announcement to clean stock

Supplier ship notices

Suppliers announce what is coming, carton by carton, before the truck arrives.

Dock door booking

Carriers book door windows of up to four hours, and no door is ever double-booked.

Carton-aware receiving

Teams scan whole cases and print carton labels instead of counting every unit.

Discrepancy catching

Short, over and damaged goods surface the moment a receipt closes.

Clean stock handover

Only good units become stock; damaged ones are recorded but never booked.

Local reference copies

Product and door data are copied locally, so receiving never waits on another team.

ASN registration and lifecycle: register/cancel supplier announcements with lines numbered 1..n (unique SKUs) and an SSCC carton tree (ADR 0012); Registered -> Receiving -> Closed or Cancelled Dock appointment scheduling: book carrier door windows (max 4 h, no overlap per door via the Schedule invariant), check-in from 30 min before the window, complete or cancel Receiving and carton-aware scans: open a receipt (ASN snapshot copied in), receive lines, case-level scans that count cases via a local packaging view (pack level -> eaches factor, ADR 0013), printable carton labels, over-receipt allowed Discrepancy management: Short/Over/Damaged found at receipt close; CartonOverLimit (configurable weight/longest-side limits), mixed-carton audit count and CartonDiscrepancyRaised as part of the receipt read model (ADRs 0014-0015) Handover to inventory-storage: ReceiptLineReceived is the published-language contract; Damaged units are recorded but never booked as receivable stock (ADR 0003) Local-copy ingestion: consume product-master ProductRegistered and pack-level events plus facility-layout LocationSlot events into known_skus / dock_doors local copies — no live cross-context lookup in either direction (ADR 0003) Analytics read side: receiving-performance report (per UTC day, optional from/to, max 366-day window) and freshness (as_of/lag_seconds) from a separate analytical Postgres fed by a dedicated projector (ADR 0006) Read-only agent surfaces: MCP server with 7 tools (ADR 0005) and an A2A agent with 2 skills — asn_receiving_status and dock_schedule (ADR 0016); all writes stay on REST
